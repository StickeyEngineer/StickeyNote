# Stickey Notes v1.0.1

This macOS update adds bulk note actions and appearance settings, improves links and checklist editing, and fixes a crash when restoring floating desktop notes.

## What's new

- **Select and manage multiple notes.** Click, hold, and drag across note cards, or choose **Select** and click individual notes. Use **Actions** to move notes to a group, lock or unlock them, keep them on the desktop, or move them to Recently Deleted. Deleted notes can also be restored together.
- **Selection shortcuts.** Command/Shift-click toggles a note; Command/Shift-drag adds to your selection. In Select mode, **Select all** (⌘A) selects the current search/filter results. Drag the selection count onto a sidebar group to move the batch, and press Escape or **Done** to clear the selection.
- **Light, Dark, and Auto themes.** Choose a theme in Settings. Auto follows your Mac's appearance, your choice is remembered, and notes keep their chosen colors.
- **Clickable links.** Website and email addresses are detected in notes and checklists. Editing an automatically detected address updates its destination, and links are preserved when saving rich text.
- **Improved checklist editing.** A native text editor keeps focus on newly added or reordered items and scrolls them into view. Return creates the next item; Command-Return toggles completion; Command-Up/Down moves an item; deleting an empty item returns focus to a neighboring item.

## Fixes

- Fixed a window layout loop that could crash the app when floating desktop notes were restored at launch.
- Improved link clicking while preserving text selection and editing, and prevented the same click from opening a link twice.

## Download and install

- Requires **macOS 14 Sonoma or later**.
- Download `Stickey-Notes-v1.0.1-macOS-universal.zip` for Apple silicon or Intel Macs.
- Extract the ZIP and move **Stickey Notes.app** to **Applications**.
- To upgrade from v1.0.0, quit Stickey Notes before replacing the existing app, then reopen it. This release uses the same local notes location and Keychain identity.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the download's SHA-256 checksum.

## Notes

This download stores notes locally. iCloud sync and WidgetKit widgets are not included.

Bulk unlocking uses Apple's system authentication prompt and preserves each note's auto-lock timer. Notes with older PIN/password locks are skipped; open their lock settings and switch to Apple authentication to use bulk unlock. Those older locks remain supported when opening notes individually.

Apple-locked notes require the Keychain credential on the Mac where they were locked. Credentials are not included in exported backups and cannot be recovered if lost.
